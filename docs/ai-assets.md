Constraints: imaginary site with no real airport names, no map labels, no text, no logos, no watermark, no people in close-up, no embedded diagrams. ## Technical drawings and text

The six archived figures in figures/ were drawn deterministically from tools/generate_figures.py and the original design.json model. They have editable sources; labels are vector text rather than generated bitmap text. They describe the earlier 600 × 400 m concept and are excluded from the current schema2 report. The version1 generator deliberately refuses the current model. English labels preserve the archived geometry and quantities.

AI assisted with text, calculations, code, and the English translation. Authors remain responsible for checking content, sources, and requirements and for following the university's disclosure rules.
