\section{Sources and design basis}
Review date: 22 October 2026. Sources distinguish official law, manufacturer specifications, and project assumptions. The open 2026 consolidated Labour Code and the 2024/2026 fire-regulation amendments were checked separately. Full standards compliance is not inferred from a standards catalogue or an EN 54/EN 50131 marking on an individual device.

FAS compatibility is based on one ACSP family, its datasheets, and the available user manual. Reading of the access-restricted new Polish installer manual 03/26 or every provision of complete placement standards is not claimed. Geometric checking radii, reserves, minimum checking voltages, and test thresholds are explicitly project assumptions. Requirements for actual construction documentation, approvals, and verification of supplied device versions remain separate from this academic design.
