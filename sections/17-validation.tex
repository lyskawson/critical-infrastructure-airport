\section{Design checks and acceptance plan}
Automated checks cover unique IDs and references, coordinate frames, room outlines, device locations, port endpoints, cable polylines and lengths, L1 order, addresses, isolators, IDS inputs, ACS channels, image angles and density, geometric coverage at the adopted radii, and walks to call points through openings. Budgets cover PoE, copper, bandwidth, recording, buffers, AC loads, batteries, charging, and voltage drop. Register and table freshness is checked against exact data exports, not merely PDF existence.

These checks do not establish construction or legal compliance. Project radii do not replace complete FAS placement rules. Optics explicitly assume an ideal model with a border reserve, without measured calibration; not all smoke behaviour, sound propagation, or moving occlusion is calculated. No table parameter is presented as a field-test result.

R1 extends checks to the full clear openings of all claimed ACS verification points, vertical framing of the AI line and region, and defined PATH1 body parts. The previous 15 negative tests are retained; five targeted cases were added: an occupied space blocking entry, D01 beyond the frame limit, AI-line feet below the image, PATH1 upper-body clipping, and an independently inflated downstream-reader source voltage. Each new case must be rejected for the intended reason, not an unrelated dependency error.

Site-dependent tasks remain before implementation acceptance: ground and installation measurement, views, audibility and accessibility tests, detector tests with appropriate smoke/heat tools, electrical-shock protection, autonomous power, and approval by the competent people. This does not alter model counts, addresses, channels, or routes. A failed test requires an explicit model correction and recalculation of dependencies.

The operator checks alarms, disablements, recording, and power daily. Inspection intervals follow device instructions and regulations: at least annually for fire-protection equipment, or sooner where instructions require it. Every intervention affecting the panel, battery, egress path, or key profile ends with a test of the relevant function.\cite{L-FIRE}
