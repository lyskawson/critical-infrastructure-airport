\subsection{Cables and functional separation}
Installation-cable purchase totals \CableTotal{} m, summing individually rounded cuts. This is not the length of one site route. Internal battery, card, and disk connections and patch cords already listed as accessories are not counted again.

\CableBom

CAT-I and CAT-O denote indoor and outdoor Category 6A copper. RS485/RS485-O carry OSDP data only; DC-READER/DC-READER-O supply readers and DC-KD supplies strikes. ALARM covers PIR and sounder circuits; SIGNAL covers contacts, information signals, and RPO; BUS-I is the actual SATEL keypad/ETHM bus. FIRE is the FAS loop. AC, AC-UPS, and AC-F are separate power cables with assigned cross-sections and functions. DP and USB are factory leads for local devices; PE denotes bonding. No aggregate unparameterised ACS/IDS cable item is used.

Ducts, trays, FAS supports, and accessories are itemised separately. A heated outdoor enclosure is unnecessary because the gate controller is indoors. There is no purchase of fibre, optics, generator, automatic transfer switch, G01 drive, voice alarm, PSP transmission equipment, or unassigned FAS modules. Their absence follows the selected scope and resolved functions, rather than hidden material allowances. Earthworks concern only the defined ducts and C07 pole base; ground conditions and existing services require measurement before implementation.
